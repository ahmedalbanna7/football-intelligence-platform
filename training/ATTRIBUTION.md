# Dataset Attribution

The training datasets are not committed to this repository. Users download
their own copies from Roboflow Universe and must preserve the attribution and
license metadata included in each export.

The current reproducible training plan uses:

- [football-players-detection](https://universe.roboflow.com/roboflow-jvuqo/football-players-detection-3zvbc/dataset/20),
  version 20, provided by a Roboflow user, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- [football-ball-detection](https://universe.roboflow.com/roboflow-jvuqo/football-ball-detection-rejhg/dataset/4),
  version 4, provided by a Roboflow user, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- [football-field-detection](https://universe.roboflow.com/roboflow-jvuqo/football-field-detection-f07vi/dataset/17),
  version 17, provided by a Roboflow user, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- [player_and_others](https://universe.roboflow.com/peter-noo1c/player_and_others/dataset/1),
  version 1, provided by a Roboflow user, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Immutable archive hashes and the derived-dataset policies are recorded in
`config/datasets.yaml`.
